Public Document Intelligence for India

Making Public Governance Open, Legible & Actionable

Orgpedia enables citizens, researchers, journalists, and public administrators to understand the inner workings of Indian government institutions. We solve the hard computational problems of extracting, structuring, and verifying public knowledge from millions of opaque government orders, gazette notifications, and resolutions published across Indian languages.

All extracted data, extraction code, and analysis tools are open-source. All information is presented in English and all22 official Indian languagesrecognized in the Eighth Schedule of the Indian Constitution.

Volunteer & Collaborate with Orgpedia

Orgpedia is an open-source, non-profit civic technology initiative. We are building digital public infrastructure for India, and we need your expertise.

🗣

Indic Language Reviewers

Help verify extracted names, designations, and tables in Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Gujarati, Odia, Punjabi, and other Eighth Schedule languages.

💻

Open Source Developers

Contribute to Python extraction pipelines, PDF geometry tools, Pydantic schemas, OCR/VLM benchmark harnesses, and interactive frontend data visualizations.

🔍

Researchers & Journalists

Use our open datasets for investigative reporting, academic research into executive governance, or policy analysis on administrative postings.
