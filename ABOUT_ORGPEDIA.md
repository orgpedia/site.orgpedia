## 2. Short Blurb (50–75 words — For Newsletters, Event Bios & Partner Pages)

> **Orgpedia Foundation** is a registered Section 8 non-profit civic technology initiative dedicated to public data transparency in India. Using automated document intelligence, OCR, and AI pipelines, Orgpedia extracts, structures, and translates public knowledge from millions of opaque government resolutions, gazette notifications, and cabinet orders into open, machine-readable datasets across English and official Indian languages. Explore open datasets and interactive portals at [orgpedia.in](https://www.orgpedia.in).

## 3. Comprehensive Summary (150 words — For Articles, Press & Directory Profiles)

> **Orgpedia Foundation** is an independent, open-source Section 8 non-profit organization dedicated to building digital public infrastructure for Indian governance data.
>
> Indian public administration generates millions of critical records—such as Cabinet orders, Government Resolutions (GRs), and legislative proceedings—frequently published as degraded scans across multiple Indic languages. Orgpedia solves the hard computational challenges of extracting, structuring, and verifying entities from these documents to make government operations transparent and accessible to citizens, researchers, and journalists.
>
> Flagship initiatives include:
> - **Ministers of India (1947–Present):** Complete tenure and portfolio history across 22 official Indian languages ([ministry.orgpedia.in](https://ministry.orgpedia.in)).
> - **Weekly Maharashtra GR Summaries:** Automated tracking and digest of state administrative decisions ([mahsummary.orgpedia.in](https://mahsummary.orgpedia.in)).
> - **indicGovBench-Orders:** A benchmark dataset evaluating AI and vision-language models on Indian government orders ([indicgovbench.orgpedia.in](https://indicgovbench.orgpedia.in)).
>
> All extraction code, tools, and datasets are released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0) license.
